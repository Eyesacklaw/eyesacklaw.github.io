Observations 《上市》

Thoughts on the market...

Observations is a weekly market summary where I digest (or at least, try to analyse) what's happening to our economy, investigating industries, rates, commodities, and whatever else catches my eye - and where I think we're headed. There is no fixed format, just whatever I think is worth talking about.

股票“上市”，是將公司公諸於世，讓人評估、讓市場定價；此博客的目標也一樣，透過分析每週的新聞發展、世界經濟狀態、股市升跌、 利率、大宗商品、債券波幅，嘗試披露市場走向，及其對我們投資者的影響。格式不定，內容隨市而行。
